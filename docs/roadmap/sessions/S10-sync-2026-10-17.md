# S10-sync source integration — 2026-10-17

Status: **source/host/build complete; not uploaded or physically validated**.

## Scope delivered

- Firmware BLE queue accepts the frozen 16-byte opcode 5 frame.
- The production adapter parses and deduplicates TimeSync, calls `LifeCore::sync`, and returns result 4 for invalid or rollback syncs.
- Capability bit 4 is advertised with stated-preference bit 5 (`0x003f`); Hatch bit 6 remains off.
- Serial reports TimeSync result, clock quality, day part and timezone offset.
- Mobile sends phone UTC/timezone after connect and every six hours, cancelling the schedule on disconnect/close.
- The simulator does not claim TimeSync because it has no wall-clock/day-part model.
